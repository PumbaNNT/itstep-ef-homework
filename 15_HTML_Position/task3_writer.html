William Shakespeare

Коротка біографія

William Shakespeare (1564 - 1616) was an English playwright, poet and actor. He is considered one of the most important writers in the English language, and his plays are still staged and studied all over the world more than four centuries after his death.

He grew up in Stratford-upon-Avon. At eighteen he married Anne Hathaway, and the couple had three children. Later he moved to London, where he worked as an actor and writer and became a co-owner of the theatre company known first as the Lord Chamberlain's Men and later as the King's Men.

His early work consists mostly of comedies and history plays. Around the turn of the seventeenth century he wrote his best-known tragedies - Hamlet, Othello, King Lear and Macbeth - and towards the end of his career he turned to tragicomedies, sometimes working together with other playwrights.

Many of his plays were printed during his lifetime. In 1623 two of his fellow actors published a collected edition of his plays, now called the First Folio, which preserved many works that might otherwise have been lost.

Over the following centuries his plays have been adapted again and again for theatre, opera, film and television, and new generations of readers and scholars keep finding fresh meanings in them.

He also wrote 154 sonnets and two long narrative poems. Many phrases from his works have become everyday expressions in English, and his characters are recognised far beyond the theatre.
